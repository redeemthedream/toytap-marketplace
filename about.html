What is Toy Tap?

Toy Tap is a price tracking and comparison platform built specifically for toys. We monitor Amazon prices daily, calculate proprietary TT Grade quality ratings, and help parents, gift-givers, and toy enthusiasts find the best deals on the toys they care about.

The toy market is enormous and prices change constantly. A toy that was $29.99 yesterday might be $19.99 today and $34.99 tomorrow. Without tracking, you have no way to know whether the price you see right now is a good deal or an inflated markup. Toy Tap solves this by building a complete price history for every product we track, then scoring each toy with our TT Grade system so you can make informed decisions at a glance.

Whether you are shopping for a birthday gift, stocking up during a sale, or just curious about whether a toy is worth its price, Toy Tap gives you the data to shop smarter.

TT Grade

Every toy in our database receives a TT Grade: a letter rating from S (exceptional) to D (poor) that summarizes its overall quality and value. TT Grades are calculated algorithmically using review data, star ratings, pricing patterns, and more. No brand can pay to influence their grade.

We also calculate a TT Score (0-100) that measures current deal quality, helping you find the best value right now.

Affiliate Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Toy Tap earns from qualifying purchases. When you click a product link on our site and make a purchase on Amazon, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you. This is how we fund the service and keep it free for everyone.

Our TT Grade ratings are algorithmically generated and not influenced by affiliate relationships. A toy that generates higher affiliate commissions does not receive a higher grade. Our scoring is based entirely on publicly available data: customer reviews, star ratings, price history, and deal quality. We believe transparency about our business model builds trust, and we want you to feel confident that our recommendations are based on data, not revenue incentives.
